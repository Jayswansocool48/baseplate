COOKIE POLICY
Version 2026-10-11

Roblox Vault itself uses no cookies, web storage, tracking pixels or
fingerprinting.

The Figma tab is different: it is a small browser showing figma.com, and Figma
sets its own cookies there (to keep you signed in, and whatever else Figma's
cookie policy describes). Those cookies:
- are only created if you open the Figma tab,
- are stored only on this computer, in
  ~/.var/app/io.github.jayswansocool48.RobloxVault/data/roblox-vault/figma-web,
- are controlled by Figma: Figma's own cookie banner and settings appear inside
  the tab, where you can accept or reject them,
- are deleted by Menu -> Figma -> Sign out, by "Delete all my data...", and by
  "Uninstall Roblox Vault...".

Before the Figma tab loads for the first time, it tells you this and waits for
you to press "Open Figma". That screen is the app's cookie notice; it never
loads Figma on its own.
