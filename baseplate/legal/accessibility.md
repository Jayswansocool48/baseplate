ACCESSIBILITY
Version 2026-10-13

- Keyboard: every action has a shortcut or is reachable with Tab, arrow keys,
  Enter and F10 (menu). See Main menu -> Keyboard shortcuts.
  Alt+1, Alt+2 and Alt+3 switch between the Code, Preview and Figma tabs.
- Screen readers: every button, list and text area has a spoken name. Icon-only
  buttons also have tooltips (the app's equivalent of alt text). The app icon
  is decorative and the app's name is always shown next to it.
- Colour: nothing relies on colour alone. Unsaved files say "• unsaved", and
  sync on/off is written out in the status bar.
- Sound: every sound comes with a written message, so nothing is said by sound
  alone. Sounds follow your system's event-sound setting at first and can be
  turned off in the menu (Sound effects).
- GUI Preview: the picture can't be read by a screen reader, so a Layers list
  next to it names every element, its type and its text. Selecting a layer
  highlights it. Hovering shows the element's path and size as text.
- Figma tab: this is Figma's own website; its accessibility is up to Figma.
- Assistant: Ctrl+J opens it and puts the cursor in the message box. Enter
  sends, Shift+Enter adds a line. Replies are plain selectable text, and every
  code block's buttons (Apply, Insert, New script, Copy) have spoken names.
- Dark theme: text has at least 6:1 contrast on every surface (body text at
  least 12:1), and the accent colour has 9:1 against its label text. With your
  system's high-contrast mode on, Baseplate uses the system colours instead.
- Text size follows your system font scaling.
